Token Launch Radar

Ranks protocols that record no token yet by what a launch could be worth — each with a farm plan.

Snapshot: DefiLlama protocols

Method

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth adjustment × age adjustment

Rounded to the nearest dollar, with a $1 floor. Every input comes from the sealed snapshot.

Category multiple
Median market-cap ÷ TVL among protocols in the same category that already trade. Fewer than 3 samples → global median. Clamped to 0.05–20.
Growth adjustment
1 + clamp(7d change, −30, +30) ÷ 100 × 0.5. A +30% week adds 15%, a −30% week subtracts 15%; a missing change counts as 0.
Age adjustment
1 + 0.2 × min(1, log10(1 + days ÷ 30) ÷ log10(13)). Longer tokenless operation scores higher, capped at +20% and reached at 360 days.
Scope
CEXs, liquid staking and restaking, bridges and canonical bridges, wrapped or staked products, exchange-branded names, and versioned pools of a live-token protocol are removed: wrappers or staking forms of an existing token, not pre-token protocols.

Every row records no token in this snapshot: neither a CoinGecko id nor a ticker. That is a snapshot fact, not a claim about a project's history or plans.
